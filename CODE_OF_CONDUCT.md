# Code of conduct

Contributions are welcome from anyone, whatever their experience. Keep
discussion about the code, the hardware and the evidence.

Be polite and specific. Criticise code and behaviour rather than the person
who wrote it, and say which of your statements are tested and which are
assumptions. Respect privacy, copyright and responsible disclosure. Some
decisions will keep an unproven write path disabled, or leave out a catalogue
source whose owners have not agreed to automated access; accept them.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualised attention, deliberate
disruption, publication of private information and sustained personal attacks
are not acceptable. Do not post disk images or links to them in issues, pull
requests or discussions, and do not pressure anyone to share software or media
they do not have permission to share.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
address on their GitHub profile. The maintainer may edit or remove
contributions, limit participation, suspend access or ban a participant.
Reports are kept confidential.
